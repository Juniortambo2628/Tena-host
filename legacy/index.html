Did you know?

OTAs (Online Travel Agencies) can take up to 20% of your booking revenue — and you lose control of the guest relationship.

When guests book through OTAs, you miss an opportunity to collect their contact details, making it difficult to rebook them directly. Tena aims to bridge this gap by providing an avenue for hosts to collect guest contact details which can be used to rebook them directly.

How Tena Works

Four simple steps to capture guest data, engage them with your brand, and drive direct bookings.

Step 1
Guest Connects to WiFi & Data is Captured

Tena captures guest data through a branded splash page & stores it in our integrated CRM.

Step 2
Guest Sees Your HomePage

A customized landing page is shown with native upsell storefronts, your guidebook, direct booking link, and more.

Step 3
Guest Receives Automated Messaging

Guest automatically receives a welcome email, SMS rate & review request, stay anniversary emails, and more.

Step 4
Guest Rebooks Direct Again & Again

Guest books direct for next trip and keep coming back.
